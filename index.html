Featured Work

UX That Drives Results

  • Pampered Chef

    Learning Center Redesign

    Problem: Pampered Chef’s independent consultants run their own sales businesses, and their training lived on a clunky, overwhelming vendor platform outside their main business website. Consultants couldn’t easily find the basics they needed to learn and succeed.

    My role: UX strategy and design

    Solution: A full content audit cut everything that wasn’t business-critical, reducing categories to ease decision overload. I added a Common Challenges section built around consultants’ most frequent problems, a single home for live learning events that had been scattered across the site and emails, and estimated completion times so lessons felt achievable.

    Results: Increased traffic by 7x compared with the vendor platform. Average increase of $553 in monthly sales per consultant (+45% year over year).

  • Pampered Chef

    Search Experience Redesign

    Problem: Consultant’s Corner is where Pampered Chef’s independent sales consultants find everything they need to run their businesses. Its search often returned confusing results and had no clear flow, so finding the right tool or resource was challenging.

    My role: UX strategy and design

    Solution: I solved this by organizing results into four easy-to-scan categories: Content, Marketing Materials, Products, and Recipes.

    • Stay in place: Because products and recipes live on the public site, I added external-link icons to their thumbnails and opened them in new tabs, so consultants never lose their spot in Consultant’s Corner.
    • Use & Care guides: Consultants rely on these for customer support, so I worked around a technical constraint to make them easy to find.
    • Content grouped the way consultants talk: I split the Content category into News, Blog, Learning Center, Consultant’s Corner, and pamperedchef.com. This matches the way consultants describe their business, so the structure feels familiar and results are easier to scan.

    Results: Launched mid-October 2026. Analytics will be added soon.

  • Pampered Chef

    45th Anniversary Promotion

    Problem: To celebrate Pampered Chef’s 45th anniversary, we ran a large promotion in which consultants could earn one-of-a-kind stoneware dinnerware pieces, handcrafted in collaboration with our longstanding stoneware manufacturer. It was tied to our regular monthly Sales Rewards and Recruiter Rewards, and the challenge was building excitement without confusing consultants. The existing rewards pages weren’t built to hold all of the anniversary details, and we didn’t want to overwhelm consultants with too much information at once.

    My role: UX strategy, design, development

    Solution:

    • One central landing page: A single home for the anniversary promotion, with separate sections for the current Sales Reward and Recruiter Reward.
    • Connected pages: The existing rewards pages link back to the landing page, so the full details are always one click away without crowding those pages.
    • Storytelling over display: The pieces were one of a kind and handcrafted, so I told the story behind them and led with emotion, not just a list of rewards.

    Results: Consultants had a 24% higher achievement rate than the year before.
